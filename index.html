About

I am an Argentinian economist and PhD student in Economics at Arizona State University. My research interests are in applied theory, political economy, auctions, and contracts. Before ASU, I received my BA and MA in Economics from Universidad de San Andrés.

Outside academia, I enjoy chess, boxing, and playing the saxophone. I am always happy to hear from people with questions, comments, or a chess challenge.
